✧ questions? we've got you

good to know, bestie.

a few things renters often want to know before getting started.

what does a guarantor do?

a guarantor acts as an institutional third party that backs your lease when you do not meet a landlord's standard income or credit requirements.

how does your private guarantor matching work?

we match our clients with private guarantors from our network, so that the private guarantor is able to co-sign along with the client - similarly to if a family member or friend were to co-sign on a client's lease.

what is the 40× income requirement?

some nyc landlords use a guideline that a renter's annual income should be around 40 times the monthly rent. requirements vary by property. we can help clients with nontraditional income organize and present supporting information, but cannot change a property's criteria.

how much do you charge for a guarantor?

pricing is personalized based on a few key factors, including your monthly rent, the coverage required by the property’s operator, and your income/credit score. we only charge clients after being approved by management/landlord.

can you help me find an apartment?

for nyc clients, we offer a broker and personal assistant service. this involves setting up viewings for you in a time frame that you decide, co-ordinating with realtors and brokers, and handling applications on your behalf. all you have to do is show up to the viewing, and tell us if you like the place!

which nyc neighborhoods do you know?

we have experience placing clients in apartments in the lower east side (les), bushwick, hell's kitchen, the west village, as well as the broader nyc market.

does using fancy guarantors guarantee approval?

no. a service inquiry or potential guarantor match does not guarantee a lease, apartment, guarantor acceptance or landlord approval. decisions are made by the relevant landlord, broker, property manager and/or guarantor.

what should i have ready?

it helps to know your move-in date, approximate rent budget, desired credit score as requested by landlord/management and any other application criteria specified by landlord/management (i.e. 80x income, 720 credit, move-in 01/12).
